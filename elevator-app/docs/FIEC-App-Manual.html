What This App Does

The FIEC Elevator app manages every elevator project from first sales call to handover and ongoing maintenance. It replaces spreadsheets, group chats, and sticky notes with one system the whole team can see and update in real time.

Who Uses It

Admin / Owner
  • Full access to everything
  • Sees contract amounts and pricing
  • Manages finance, invoices, billing
  • Approves deletions and overrides
  • Views Admin Audit log
Operations Staff
  • Projects, jobs, breakdowns
  • Customer information
  • Pipeline steps assigned to them
  • No access to pricing or finance
  • Contact numbers visible when live

New Installations — The 12 Steps

Each step must be completed before the next one unlocks. Steps marked with a file icon require a document upload.

Step 1 — Project Awarded (Admin confirms)
Step 2 — Shop Drawings Prepared 📎
Step 3 — Client Signs Drawings 📎
Step 4 — Engineer Approval 📎
Step 5 — Payment to Supplier ← auto-adds to Project Status
Step 6 — Production Started (enter end date)
Step 7 — Production Monitoring
Step 8 — Shipment (tracking number)
Step 9 — Customs & Arrival
Step 10 — Installation
Step 11 — Testing 📎
Step 12 — Turnover to Client 📎

How to use: Update the status whenever something changes. Set a Next Action with a due date and assign a PIC (Person In Charge) so everyone knows who is responsible.

Maintenance Contracts (AMC)

AMC = Annual Maintenance Contract. These are the recurring agreements where clients pay FIEC to service their elevators regularly.

When a contract is within 60 days of expiry, a Renewal Tracking panel appears on the contract page. Update it as the negotiation moves forward:

Not started → Client contacted → In negotiation → Proposal sent → Renewed ✓

AMC Billing: Go to AMC Billing in the sidebar to see which clients need invoicing this month. Mark each as Billed → Paid.

  • Adds items to Dashboard when needed
